# Plan an AUMARA stay

Use public information from https://www.aumara.me/ and https://www.aumara.me/llms.txt.

## Rules

- Treat AUMARA and EL CID Country Club as separate accommodation products.
- AUMARA is independent geodesic houses (Chalet and Superior Chalet), not a bungalow.
- Current AUMARA sellable inventory is 3 Chalet units for up to 4 guests each and 2 Superior Chalet units for up to 6 guests each. A sixth physical house exists but is not presented as bookable without current PMS evidence.
- Do not invent live availability, prices, minimum-stay rules, cancellation terms, or services that are not published.
- For current availability and price, use the direct Beds24 booking links exposed by AUMARA.
- For policy or guest-information questions, prefer the canonical AUMARA site and its machine-readable guide.
- If a requested fact is not published, say that it is not confirmed on the public site.
